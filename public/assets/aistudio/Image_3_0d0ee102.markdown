## Brand & Style

This design system defines an understated, architectural digital flagship for high-end luxury fashion. Drawing inspiration from modern couture houses such as The Row, Totême, Loro Piana, and Celine, the aesthetic avoids ornamental excess in favor of structural clarity, cinematic scale, and absolute refinement.

The experience prioritizes:
- **Quiet Authority:** Generous, intentional negative space that allows garments, fabric textures, and silhouette photography to dominate the viewport.
- **Editorial Composition:** High-contrast serif headlines juxtaposed against crisp, widely spaced sans-serif technical data, evoking printed lookbooks and couture monographs.
- **Razor Precision:** Architectural lines, exact 1px structural partitions, and razor-sharp geometries that reflect precise tailoring and fabric edges.

## Colors

The palette balances warm organic undertones with deep obsidian precision, ensuring garment photography remains the primary focal point:

- **Midnight Black (`#171717`):** The primary structural tone. Used for dominant typography, structural button fills, crisp strokes, and high-contrast callouts.
- **Warm Ivory (`#F4F0E8`):** The master ambient backdrop. Replaces sterile digital white with a tactile, linen-washed warmth reminiscent of archival lookbook paper.
- **Champagne Beige (`#B49A78`):** The secondary metallic accent. Applied sparingly to active indicators, curated tags, key dividers, and quiet luxury badges.
- **Soft Taupe (`#8B8176`):** The tertiary grounding shade. Used for razor-thin 1px border structures, secondary specifications, and subtle hover interactions.
- **Pure White (`#FFFFFF`):** Reserved for product photography staging blocks, card surfaces that require clean separation, floating drawers, and modal interiors.
- **Subtle Surface Tint (`#EAE5DC`):** Used strictly for border divisions across pure ivory, input wells, and alternate table rows.

## Typography

The typographic hierarchy pairs high-fashion editorial presence with rational contemporary utility:

- **Editorial Headings (`Playfair Display`):** Utilized for collection titles, editorial banners, and major campaign statements. Italic cuts are selectively introduced for artisanal accents (e.g., *Collection*, *The Atelier*, *Made in Italy*).
- **Body & Technical Copy (`Plus Jakarta Sans`):** Balanced, geometric, and clear, rendering garment descriptions, care guidelines, and logistics clearly across high-density displays.
- **Micro-Labels & Metadata:** All navigation links, categorical badges, filters, inventory indicators, and dimension parameters are formatted in uppercase with wide letter-spacing (`0.22em`), evoking classic luxury garment tags.

## Layout & Spacing

The layout is built upon an architectural 12-column grid prioritizing deliberate pacing and expansive negative space:

- **Desktop (1200px+):** Max layout container capped at 1680px. 12 columns with a 1.5rem gutter and expansive outer margins (4rem). Product gallery spreads alternate between 2-column full-bleed lookbook pairs and single centered silhouettes with wide asymmetrical negative space.
- **Tablet (768px – 1199px):** 8 columns, 1.25rem gutter, and 2.5rem margins. Product grids collapse cleanly to 2 columns.
- **Mobile (< 768px):** 4 columns with tight 0.75rem gutters and 1.25rem horizontal safe padding. Galleries shift to an edge-to-edge swipe model with persistent hairline counter indicators.
- **Vertical Rhythm:** Sections are punctuated by intentional pauses (`space-2xl` to `space-3xl`). Spacing between product photography and technical details is kept minimal to retain clear visual associations.

## Elevation & Depth

This system rejects physical drop shadows and skeuomorphic blur stacks, establishing spatial depth through tonal separation and razor-thin outlines:

- **Zero-Shadow Philosophy:** Standard boxes, product cards, popovers, and sticky banners feature zero shadow (`box-shadow: none`).
- **Architectural Seams (1px Lines):** Structure is articulated through razor-thin borders (`1px solid #8B8176` at 30% opacity, or `1px solid #B49A78` at 40% opacity). Cards and navigation boundaries meet like fine fabric seams.
- **Tonal Layering:** 
  - Canvas: Base Warm Ivory (`#F4F0E8`).
  - Card / Lookbook Plate: Raised Pure White (`#FFFFFF`).
  - Slide-out Cart & Fitting Room Modals: Pure White (`#FFFFFF`) with a 1px Midnight Black (`#171717`) or Taupe frame, anchored over a high-opacity Midnight Black scrim (`#171717` at 65% opacity).
- **Interactive Planes:** Dropdowns and quick-buy drawers use clean, unblurred white panels framed with a single 1px Midnight Black border line.

## Shapes

Every component adheres to a sharp 0px radius profile (`roundedness: 0`). 

- **Architectural Edges:** Buttons, imagery wrappers, modals, inputs, and flyouts are cut with crisp 90-degree right angles, evoking tailored suiting, archival stationery, and rigid gift boxes.
- **Purity of Form:** No soft radii, pill buttons, or circular floating actions are permitted. Selection swatches (colors, materials) maintain square proportions with subtle inner offsets rather than standard circular tokens.

## Components

### Buttons
- **Primary:** Solid Midnight Black (`#171717`) background, Warm Ivory (`#F4F0E8`) text. Zero border radius, 0.6875rem uppercase micro-label typography with wide letter-spacing. Padding: `1.125rem 2.5rem`. Hover: Transitions to Champagne Beige (`#B49A78`) background with Midnight Black text over 250ms linear.
- **Secondary / Ghost:** Transparent background, 1px solid Midnight Black (`#171717`) border, Midnight Black text. Hover: Solid Midnight Black fill with Warm Ivory text.
- **Text Link Button:** Raw text, zero padding, accompanied by a razor-thin 1px underline offset by 6px. Hover: Underline transitions smoothly from Midnight Black to Champagne Beige.

### Cards & Lookbook Tiles
- **Product Card:** Pure White (`#FFFFFF`) or Warm Ivory base with zero drop shadow. Optional 1px border in `#8B8176` (20% opacity). 
- **Image Aspect Ratios:** Strict 4:5 or 3:4 portrait ratios. Hover state reveals an alternate silhouette angle via a 400ms crossfade, paired with a discreet "Quick View" action anchored flush at the bottom edge.
- **Meta Block:** Product title in uppercase micro-label or medium-weight sans, followed by Champagne-tinted fabric notes (e.g., *Double-Faced Cashmere*) and price rendered in numeric sans.

### Luxury Badges & Micro-Tags
- **Attributes:** Rectangular framing, 1px border in Champagne Beige (`#B49A78`), transparent background.
- **Content:** Strict uppercase 10px type (`0.25em` tracking) for details like *LIMITED EDITION*, *HAND-WOVEN*, or *MADE TO ORDER*.

### Input Fields & Selectors
- **Form Controls:** Transparent backgrounds or Pure White surfaces with a continuous 1px bottom border in `#8B8176` (standard) or a full 1px hairline box. 
- **Focus State:** Border shifts cleanly to Midnight Black (`#171717`). Placeholders display in Soft Taupe italic or light sans, transitioning to floating uppercase micro-labels upon focus.
- **Quantity & Size Pickers:** Monolithic square grids with 1px border divisions. Selected sizes are filled with Midnight Black and white text; disabled sizes display a single diagonal hairline slash.

### Checkboxes & Radios
- **Design:** Geometric square boxes (16px x 16px) bounded by a 1px Soft Taupe border.
- **Active State:** Checkbox fills with Midnight Black featuring an ultra-fine white checkmark; radio displays an inner solid square rather than a circle.

### Lists & Accordions
- **Editorial Accordion:** Applied to garment care, sizing guides, and provenance details. Bordered by 1px top and bottom hairlines (`#8B8176` at 30% opacity). Headings utilize Playfair Display with thin `+` / `−` glyphs. Smooth vertical accordion expansion.

### Shopping Drawer & Bag Tray
- Edge-to-edge slide-out panel from the right edge, styled in Pure White with a 1px Midnight Black vertical left border. Garment summary rows sit atop subtle taupe dividers, culminating in a clear, distraction-free checkout trigger.
